Glossary

Plain-English meanings of the banking and regulatory terms used on this site.

*Definitions are simplified for readability. They are not financial, legal or regulatory advice.

1

Terms

BCDGKLPRS
Banking partner
A bank that provides accounts, payments, cards or lending that a fintech offers to its own customers. It is often called a sponsor bank. The fintech builds the product on top of the bank's licence and systems.
Capital adequacy ratio
A bank's capital compared with its risk-weighted assets. A higher ratio means a bigger cushion to absorb losses.
Disclosure
Information a bank publishes about its finances, risks and governance, often because regulators require it.
Due diligence
The checks a company carries out on a partner before and during a working relationship.
Gross NPA ratio
Non-performing assets are loans that have stopped earning, usually because the borrower has not paid for 90 days or more. The gross NPA ratio is those loans as a share of all loans. A lower ratio means fewer problem loans.
Knowledge graph
A way of storing information as connected records, such as a bank linked to its regulator, its disclosures and the circulars that apply to it, so relationships can be followed directly.
Liquidity coverage ratio
The high-quality liquid assets a bank holds compared with the cash it could need in a 30-day stress period. Above 100% means it holds enough.
Pillar 3 disclosures
Reports banks publish under the international Basel framework, setting out their capital, risk exposures and how they manage risk. They are one of the public sources TrustFrame is designed to read.
Regulatory circular
An official notice from a regulator that sets or changes rules for the institutions it supervises.
Reporting period
The span of time a disclosure covers, such as a financial year. Comparing the same figure across periods shows what changed.
Risk-weighted assets
A bank's assets adjusted for risk, so riskier loans count for more. Capital ratios are measured against this figure.
Source trail
TrustFrame's term for the record of where a figure came from: the document, section, page and date. Every figure in the concept previews on this site has one.
