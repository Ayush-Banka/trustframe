How TrustFrame is designed to work

TrustFrame is being developed to turn scattered banking disclosures and regulatory information into one structured, traceable view. This page sets out the problem, the approach and the capabilities we plan to build.

*In brief: collect from authoritative sources, organize the information into connected records, keep the evidence attached, and support better banking-partner decisions.

1

Important information. Scattered across sources.

Relevant banking disclosures and regulatory information may be spread across annual reports, bank websites, regulatory circulars and other sources. Finding, checking, comparing and organizing it takes time.

1.1

Fragmented research

Disclosures, reports and updates sit in different places and formats. Building a full picture of one bank can mean checking many sources by hand.

1.2

Changes over time

Regulatory information is updated over time. Keeping track of what changed, and when, is hard when each source is reviewed on its own.

1.3

Evidence and context

A finding is most useful when you can see where it came from1 and what it relates to. Linking each point back to its source takes extra effort.

1This is the gap TrustFrame is designed around: the source travels with the information.

2

From scattered sources to structured intelligence.

Four steps, in order. Each one is designed to keep the link between a piece of information and the source it came from.

2.1

Collect

Bring relevant information from authoritative sources into a structured workflow.

2.2

Organize

Standardize information and connect related entities2, disclosures and updates.

2Entities here means banks, regulators, disclosures and regulatory events.

2.3

Understand

Review history, source evidence and connected insights.

2.4

Act with context

Support informed due diligence3 and banking-partner assessments.

3TrustFrame is designed to support decisions, not to make them. The judgement stays with your team.

Who we aim to support

Who we aim to support

TrustFrame is being designed for teams that need a clear, traceable view of the banks they work with, or plan to work with.

*The questions on this page are typical of banking-partner due diligence. They show the kind of work TrustFrame is meant to support. They are not customer quotes.

1

Fintech companies

Banking-partner research and due diligence.

Fintechs often rely on bank partners for accounts, payments, lending or other regulated services. Before and during a partnership, teams need a clear view of each partner's disclosures and regulatory context.

Questions TrustFrame aims to help answer

  • What has this bank disclosed, and where?
  • Which regulatory updates relate to this partner?
  • What has changed since our last review?
  • Can we show where each finding came from?

*Roles we have in mind: founders, partnership leads, and risk and compliance teams.

2

Enterprise teams

Risk, compliance and business teams reviewing bank information.

Enterprises review the banks they hold accounts with, borrow from or route payments through. Those reviews are easier when findings are consistent across banks and can be traced to a source.

Questions TrustFrame aims to help answer

  • Is our view of each bank consistent and complete?
  • Where does this finding come from?
  • Which banks does a new regulatory update affect?
  • What should we look at again before the next review?

*Roles we have in mind: treasury, procurement, and risk and compliance teams.

About TrustFrame

About TrustFrame

TrustFrame is an early-stage venture developing a regulatory intelligence platform for banking-partner decisions.

*Status: in development. No part of the product is live yet.

1

Why TrustFrame

Decisions about banking partners rest on information that is mostly public, but scattered.

We think that information should be easier to find, easier to compare and simple to trace back to its source1. TrustFrame is being built around that idea, for fintechs and enterprises that need to understand the banks they rely on.

1Tracing means each finding links to the document, section and date it came from.

2

Our principles

Evidence-led
Every finding should point to the evidence behind it.
Source-aware
Information keeps its source, its date and its context.
Built for clarity
Consistent structure, plain language and no unnecessary noise.

3

What we will not claim

A product about trusted information should hold its own website to the same standard.

  • No customers, partners or results that we do not have.
  • No figures without a source.
  • No product screens dressed up as real data. Exhibits on this site are concept previews built on fictional examples.

4

Where we are now
